Delete my account

You can erase your Dooka account and your personal data at any time, without having to ask anyone.

From the app

This is the quickest way: deletion is immediate.

  1. Open DookaThen tap your photo or your name at the top of the screen: the My profile page opens.
  2. Scroll right to the bottom of the pageBelow Appearance, Notifications, My limits, Settings and About.
  3. Tap “Delete my account”You’ll be asked to confirm twice: “Continue”, then “Delete for good”.

If you no longer have the app

Email us from the email address linked to your Dooka account — that’s how we check it’s really yours. Your request will be handled within 30 days, in practice much sooner.

Request deletion by email

What gets erased

  • Your profile: name, email address, photo
  • Your profile photo
  • Your stats, and the tickets you logged outside a session, with their photos
  • Your friends, your place in your crews, your suggestions and your votes
  • Your notification token

One exception. Your tickets played in a session stay on record, under “Deleted player”, with their proof photos. Since version 3.10, that applies to all your tickets, even ones played alone: each one goes through a session. Otherwise, the totals of a night out for five would change after the fact for the four other players.

This record no longer includes your name, your email or your profile photo. Crews you created, and their images, stay for their other members.

It’s permanent. There’s no bin and no cooling-off period. Creating a new account with the same email address or the same Google or Apple account will give you a brand-new, completely empty account.
