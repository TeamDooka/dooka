Reporting content

Dooka hosts what its users post: usernames, photos, crew names, suggestions, information about bars. To report content you believe is illegal or breaks the terms of use:

  • from the app, with the report button on profiles, crews, bar pages, ticket photos and suggestions;
  • or by email to contact@dooka.fr, saying which content you mean, where it is, why you’re reporting it, your name and an address where we can reply to you.

This address is also Dooka’s single point of contact for the authorities and for users. You can write to us in French or in English. What happens after a report is described in section 7 of the terms of use.
